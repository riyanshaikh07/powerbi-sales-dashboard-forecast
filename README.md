# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Super Store sales data to understand **sales, profit, delivery performance and regional trends**, along with a short-term **15-day sales forecast**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

- Track overall **Sales, Profit, Quantity and Delivery Days**
- Find top-performing **categories, sub-categories and states**
- Understand sales by **customer segment, ship mode and payment mode**
- Forecast sales for the next **15 days**

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

- **File:** `SuperStore_Sales_DataSet.xlsx`
- **Records:** 5,901 rows
- **Period:** Jan 2019 – Dec 2020
- **Key columns:** Order Date, Ship Mode, Segment, Region, State, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Profit, Payment Mode

---

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Sales Dashboard
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity, Delivery Days
- Region slicer for quick filtering
- Sales by Category and Sub-Category
- Sales by Segment and Payment Mode (donut charts)
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales & Profit trend over time (area charts)
- Sales by State (map)

### Page 2 – Sales Forecast
- 15-day sales forecast (line chart)
- Sales trend over time
- Sales by State
